# Brag Plan: Orion

## What is this app?
Orion turns newly registered business data into enriched, searchable leads: pick an industry and a place, and see fresh companies with their decision-makers and contact channels.

## The angle
The window to reach a new business is short. The video is one uninterrupted product moment: type a search, watch fresh companies resolve with decision-maker intelligence, unlock one, and land on "Reach them before everyone else." No mockup diagrams, only the working search flow.

## Hook (0-4s)
Near-black frame. "Every day, new businesses open." fades in and holds, then dissolves as a search bar types "Manufacturing · Tamil Nadu · last 30 days" and its filter chips lock in.

## Key moments
- Results counter ticks up to 1,284 new businesses while four company cards arrive one by one.
- Each card carries a decision-maker (name, role) and verified Phone / Email / LinkedIn channels.
- One company opens into a detail panel; a cursor clicks "Unlock contact" and the number resolves.

## Outro / punchline
"Reach them before everyone else." Orion wordmark, "Newly registered. Fully enriched."

## User flow worth showing
Search (industry + location + age) -> scan enriched results -> open one and unlock the decision-maker's contact.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, premium, cinematic product film; dark and data-driven
- Interpretation: few scenes, confident holds, soft crossfades and slow ease-outs; motion is smooth and slightly futuristic (glow, scan line) but never busy.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20s

## Visual identity
- Background: #05070B
- Accent: #5CE1E6 (single cool cyan)
- Text: #E8EEF5, muted #7C8A9B
- Display font: Inter (project font), tight tracking
- Body font: Inter; JetBrains Mono for data
- Strongest visual element: the landing page "Example Search" (Manufacturing, Tamil Nadu, last 30 days) and its result cards. Company names come from that sample; decision-makers are fictional stand-ins.

## Share copy (draft)
New businesses open every day. Orion shows you who they are and who to call, while they are still easy to reach.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a steady bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (114.84 BPM), fade in from 0, fade out over last 1.5s
- Music treatment: low-to-moderate volume, ducked feel under the outro
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues: 4.23s (results reveal), 12.65s (focus on one lead), 17.38s (outro line). Beat grid 5.28, 6.34, 7.40, 8.44 for the four cards (every other beat, ~1.06s apart).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; background glow breathes on the beat grid (deterministic, no waveform visuals)
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched: soft card sound per card, one click on Unlock
- Audio-coupled moments: typed query, four-card sequence, unlock click
- Restraint rule: no risers, no impacts louder than the bed

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Search — 4.2s
"Every day, new businesses open." holds ~2.6s, then the search field types the query; three filter chips appear.
Sequential/interaction: yes — query types character by character; chips appear one by one (hold >= 0.8s each).
Audio intent: calm anticipation.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (search bar glides to top, locked to 4.23s)

### Scene 2 — Results — 8.4s
Header "1,284 new businesses" counts up. Four cards arrive on alternate beats. Each: company, location, "Registered N days ago", decision-maker with channel pills.
Sequential/interaction: yes — four cards, one per ~1.06s, full set held ~4s afterward.
Audio intent: momentum with clarity.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Unlock — 4.7s
One card stays, the rest recede. A detail panel shows company status, type, website, and the decision-maker's masked number. Cursor clicks "Unlock contact"; the number resolves.
Sequential/interaction: yes — simulated click.
Audio intent: small payoff.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Outro — 2.7s
"Reach them before everyone else." Orion mark and "Newly registered. Fully enriched."
Sequential/interaction: none.
Audio intent: settle; music fades out.

**Music mood for this video:** cinematic-calm business bed
**Audio summary:** steady bed, three restrained card/click accents, fade out on the outro.

# Brag Plan: Orion (launch teaser, product not shown)

## What is this app?
Orion turns newly registered business data into enriched, searchable leads with decision-maker intelligence.

## The angle
A cinematic launch teaser that never shows the product UI. The Orion constellation assembles star by star on the beat, becomes the Orion logo, then lands on "A new star is rising. Launching soon."

## Hook (0-3.4s)
Black frame, one star pulses. "Every day, new businesses open."

## Key moments
- Seven stars ignite on the music's beat; lines draw between them to form the constellation. "Be the first to find them."
- Camera pushes through the belt; the constellation dissolves into the real Orion logo with a light sweep and shock ring.
- Logo lifts; "A new star is rising." then "Discover. Enrich. Act." then "Launching soon."

## Outro
"Business intelligence for modern sales teams." Launching soon pill.

## Tone
- Preset: cinematic (user asked for "launching style, not simple")
- Direction: premium launch teaser, restrained, no product UI, no invented claims, no launch date.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080 | Duration: 18s

## Visual identity
Background #0A0A0A, text #FFFFFF, muted #A1A1AA, accent #7C3AED (text tint #A78BFA). Inter + JetBrains Mono. Real logo from ownus/public/black.png.

## Audio
Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-9 (114.84 BPM). Star ignitions on beat-grid 4.23-7.40 (every beat, non-text). Strong cues locked: 4.23 (first ignition), 8.44 (camera push), 10.54 (logo), 12.65 (headline). Soft star ticks, one logo accent. Subtle glow breathes on the beat grid.
